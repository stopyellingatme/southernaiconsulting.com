What we don't do

A short list keeps us good at a narrow thing. Here's what we turn down — every time, including for people we like.

  • General tech support and break/fix. Printers, Wi-Fi, email outages, "my computer is slow," network setup. We're glad to refer you to a local IT shop we trust.
  • Websites, hosting, SEO, and social media management. Not our trade.
  • Ongoing IT management or being on call for systems that aren't ours.
  • Work without a written scope and a cleared deposit. It protects both of us.
  • Anything touching patient health data or other HIPAA-regulated information.
  • AI that makes high-stakes calls on its own — legal, medical, or financial determinations. AI assists your experts; it doesn't replace their judgment.

How we handle your data

  • You own your data and your accounts. Wherever possible we build inside your tools, under your logins, so nothing depends on us.
  • Business-grade AI tools only. We work in business and enterprise tiers, where your data is not used to train AI models.
  • We collect only what the project needs. No sensitive personal information we don't require, and never regulated data.
  • We delete our working copies of your files after delivery and handoff.
  • AI makes mistakes. Anything AI generates should be reviewed by a person before you rely on it. We build in guardrails; you stay in control of what goes out the door.

Questions about your data? Ask us. We'd rather over-explain.
